This technical specification applies to the output shaft of gearbox R-160, drawing number 45.67.89, item code RD-450789 (hereinafter "the product"), manufactured by Rodos-Detal Group at its Kovrov site. The specification is used for export contract KMP-2025/02 with ТОО «KazMashParts» (Republic of Kazakhstan) and for other export deliveries of the product, together with the Russian-language original TU-45-67-89.

## 1. Technical requirements

1.1. The product is made of steel grade 40Х (GOST 4543-2016); blank — hot-rolled round bar, diameter 80 mm, length 420 mm.

1.2. The bearing journal diameter is machined to tolerance field ø60 k6.

1.3. Radial runout of the bearing journals shall not exceed 0.03 mm.

1.4. Surface roughness of the mating surfaces — Ra 1.25 μm.

1.5. Hardness of the induction-hardened journals — 45…50 HRC.

1.6. Product weight — 11.4 kg.

1.7. No permanent coating is applied; for long-term storage the product is preserved with oil К-17 in accordance with quality-control method OTK-MI-008.

1.8. Marking includes the part designation, batch number and the quality-control stamp; placement and method are set out in the process route card.

1.9. Delivery set: the product in preservation packaging, together with a quality certificate.

1.10. The product surface shall be free of dents, cracks, corrosion and impact marks; isolated tooling marks that do not push the dimension outside the tolerance field are acceptable.

1.11. The product is manufactured at the Kovrov site along the route: turning, milling of the keyway, induction hardening, cylindrical grinding, quality-control inspection.

## 2. Safety requirements

2.1. The product presents no hazard during storage and transportation in standard packaging.

2.2. Installation and removal of the product within the gearbox shall follow the gearbox operating manual and the lifting rules applicable to loads over 10 kg.

## 3. Acceptance rules

3.1. The manufacturer's quality-control department accepts the product by batch on the basis of the accompanying documentation.

3.2. The buyer's incoming inspection by quantity and quality is performed under the acceptance procedure set out in the supply contract.

3.3. Claims are submitted to the supplier under the terms of contract KMP-2025/02, stating the batch number.

3.4. Sample inspection of dimensions at batch acceptance by the manufacturer covers not less than 5 % of the batch, and not less than three units; if a nonconformity is found in any unit, the sample size is doubled.

## 4. Control methods

4.1. Linear dimensions and tolerance fields are controlled under method OTK-MI-001.

4.2. Radial runout and shaft geometry are controlled under method OTK-MI-002.

4.3. Hardness after heat treatment is controlled under method OTK-MI-003.

4.4. Surface roughness is controlled under method OTK-MI-004.

## 5. Transportation and storage

5.1. The product may be transported by any covered mode of transport that prevents mechanical damage and moisture ingress.

5.2. For export deliveries under contract KMP-2025/02 the product is delivered DAP Almaty.

5.3. The product is stored in a closed, heated warehouse at a temperature not below 5 °C and relative humidity not exceeding 70 %.

5.4. Storage life without re-preservation does not exceed 12 months from the shipment date.

## 6. Operating instructions

6.1. Installation into the R-160 gearbox is performed in accordance with the gearbox operating manual.

6.2. Mating surfaces are cleaned of preservation coating and inspected for damage before installation.

6.3. Operation of the product outside the rated conditions of the R-160 gearbox is not permitted.

6.4. If the bearing units are removed during gearbox maintenance, it is recommended to re-check the radial runout of the shaft journals before reassembly.

## 7. Manufacturer's warranty

7.1. The warranty period for the product is 12 months from the shipment date.

7.2. The warranty does not cover failures caused by improper installation, operation outside rated conditions, or mechanical damage.

7.3. Warranty obligations terminate if the product is used together with equipment that does not conform to the R-160 gearbox operating documentation.
